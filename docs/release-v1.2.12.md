The splash counts the games on your home server.

## Fixed

- **The splash stopped at "00 / 01"** with games on a home server: its total counted only what is on the USB, and
  the server's games were never added. They are now counted as the catalog arrives (and HD Loader games too).
  The line now reads "Cargando juegos" / "Loading games": it is the game list, covers load later as they show.

## Updating

With the network cable in, START at the update notice. Otherwise copy `launcher.elf` **and** `neutrino/` from the
zip over the ones on your drive.
